Jeep Carputer Screens

Nine cards at true 240×320 for the Waveshare ESP32-S3 panel — a welcome screen, five swipeable peers, one drill-down, one pre-mapping state, one overlay. Zoom to judge legibility; these are life size.

HORIZONTAL AXIS - the five peers, wrapping. This is the only axis a moving driver uses: a thumb flick anywhere on the panel, no aiming.

After 30s untouched while the vehicle is moving, it animates back to DRIVE. Parked, it stays put.

Swipe UP on any card for its detail view. Long-press the status bar for 1s to reach settings and diagnostics - deliberately the hardest gesture.

NO NUMBER LIES ABOUT ITS AGE

ATMA delivers only ~61 of ~168 frames/sec, so gaps are normal. Every value past its TTL dims and reverts to dashes rather than silently holding. Nothing here has talked to a real car yet, which makes a frozen-but-plausible number the worst failure mode available - it is the one that gets believed.
