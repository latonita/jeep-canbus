True 240×320, real screens, not swatches. Every ramp is built in OKLCH at hue 151° — the same hue the shipping palette actually sits at — mirroring the night ramp's lightness spacing so the steps keep their perceptual distance. Chroma is a fraction of what the gamut allows at each lightness, which is what stops the mid-tones going grey. Judge these in sun, not at a desk.
